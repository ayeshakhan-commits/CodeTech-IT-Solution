=====================================================================
DATA DICTIONARY: SMART PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION ANALYTICS
=====================================================================

1. Route_ID
   - Description: Unique identifier assigned to each public transit route.
   - Type: Text / Alphanumeric (e.g., R-101)

2. Route_Name
   - Description: The specific start and endpoints defining the transit route.
   - Type: Text (e.g., Route A (Downtown - Airport))

3. Bus_Number
   - Description: Unique registration and fleet code assigned to each bus.
   - Type: Text / Alphanumeric (e.g., BUS-4321)

4. Bus_Type
   - Description: Classification of the operational fleet vehicle.
   - Type: Categorical (Double Decker, Standard Electric, Articulated Bus, Mini Bus)

5. Date
   - Description: The date on which the schedule/trip was operated.
   - Type: Date (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)

6. Departure_Time
   - Description: The recorded scheduled departure time of the bus from its origin station.
   - Type: Time (Format: HH:MM)

7. Delay_Duration_Minutes
   - Description: Total duration of delays logged during transit (Calculated as 0 for On-Time or Cancelled trips).
   - Type: Integer

8. Passenger_Count
   - Description: Total volume of commuters/passengers boarding the bus during the individual trip.
   - Type: Integer

9. Ticket_Revenue
   - Description: Gross financial earnings calculated from ticket sales during the ride.
   - Type: Decimal / Currency (PKR)

10. Fuel_Consumption_Liters
    - Description: Volume of fuel or equivalent energy consumed by the vehicle during the specified route.
    - Type: Decimal

11. Driver_ID
    - Description: Unique identity reference assigned to the transit operator/driver.
    - Type: Text / Alphanumeric (e.g., DRV-502)

12. Weather_Condition
    - Description: Environmental/weather status logged at the time of transit.
    - Type: Categorical (Clear, Rainy, Foggy, Overcast)

13. Day_Type
    - Description: Operational classification of the calendar day.
    - Type: Categorical (Weekday, Weekend)

14. Trip_Status
    - Description: Final operational status of the scheduled transport trip.
    - Type: Categorical (On Time, Delayed, Cancelled)
